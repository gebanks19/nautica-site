Easy Days: privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Easy Days is a word search game for Android and iPhone made by Grab It.

We collect no data. Easy Days does not send any personal or usage information to Grab It or to anyone else, and we do not collect, share or sell any. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not use the internet.

Your progress. So you can carry on where you left off, Easy Days saves your current puzzle, your recent word lists and your settings (such as text size, high contrast, music and sounds) in its own storage on your device. Grab It never receives them. Depending on your device's backup and transfer settings, your phone or tablet may include them in its own backups or copy them to a new device, as it does for other apps; those backups are managed by your device's provider (such as Google or Apple) under your settings, not by us. Uninstalling Easy Days removes the copy on your device.

Children. Easy Days is made for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes. If this ever changes, for example if we add advertising, we will update this page and the app's store listing before the change reaches you.
