Ludo Reign

Classic Ludo, where the dice decide.

Ludo Reign is Classic Ludo for iPhone and Android: quick online games, private tables with friends, games against the computer, and pass-and-play on one phone.

  • Every roll is random. No paid dice, no power-ups, no outcome tuning.
  • Computer players are always labelled. They carry a bot icon and an avatar only bots use.
  • Money buys looks, never wins. The shop sells boards, dice, pawns, frames and emotes, all of which change only how the game looks.

Play with a friend

  1. Open Ludo Reign and tap Friends.
  2. Make a table, and share its six-letter code (for example K7M2QX).
  3. Your friend opens Ludo Reign, taps Friends, and types the code.

Closed beta

Ludo Reign is in a closed beta with a small group of invited testers. It isn’t in the App Store or Google Play yet. Testers get it through TestFlight (iPhone) or a Google Play test link (Android).
