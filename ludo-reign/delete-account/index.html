Delete your Ludo Reign account

Ludo Reign is developed and published by Grab It US LLC (shown as ‘Grab It US’ on Google Play).

The quickest way: in the app

  1. Open Ludo Reign and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Changing phones? You don’t need to delete anything. Link Sign in with Apple or Google in Profile, then sign in with it on your new phone. Sign in with Apple is on iPhone only, so it moves your progress to another iPhone.

What is deleted

  • Your progress: coins, trophies, Crown XP, level, statistics and daily bonus streak.
  • Your items, and the looks you chose.
  • Your generated name, avatar and flag.
  • Your weekly ranking entry.
  • Your linked Apple or Google sign-in, including the email address that came with it.
  • Your coin history and purchase history.

We also revoke your Sign in with Apple link at Apple and delete your record at RevenueCat, our purchase-processing service. If either can’t be done straight away, we keep trying until it’s done.

What is kept, without anything that links to you

  • Game records: we keep a record of every move, so games can be checked for fairness. Your player ID and name in them are replaced with “Deleted player” and a new random ID.
  • Purchase records: a copy with no player ID is kept for refunds and accounting.
  • Rewarded-ad records: the coins, day and time, with no player ID, so an ad can’t be paid twice.

Backups

Encrypted copies of our database are kept for up to about 31 days, then deleted. Your account may remain in these copies until they expire. They’re used only to recover the game after a fault.

Coins, items and Remove ads

Deleting your account doesn’t refund purchases. Coins and items can’t be restored after deletion. Remove ads can be restored: tap Restore purchases in the Shop while signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account you bought it with, on the same kind of phone.

If you can’t use the app

Email ludoreign@nautica.ky with the subject “Delete my account” and include:

  • your Player ID, from Settings → About in the app (tap to copy it). If you can’t open the app any more, tell us your in-game name and roughly when you last played;
  • whether you linked Sign in with Apple or Google. If you did, we’ll email the address that came with it (with Apple, this may be a private relay address that forwards to your own email) to check the request is yours, and you reply to it. Nothing is deleted until you do.

We need to check the account is yours before we delete it. Your Player ID tells us which account you mean, but it isn’t proof that it’s yours. We may ask you for:

  • a screenshot of Settings → About and Profile from the app;
  • or details only the account’s owner would know, such as your coins or level.

If we can’t confirm it’s yours, we’ll explain why and won’t delete the account. We’ll reply within 30 days, and usually much sooner. Once deleted, the account can’t be recovered.
