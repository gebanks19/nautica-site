Email ludoreign@nautica.ky and tell us:

  • your Player ID, from Settings → About in the app (tap it to copy);
  • the app version, shown on the same screen;
  • your phone (for example “iPhone XR” or “Pixel 7”);

Common questions

I’m getting a new phone. How do I keep my progress? On your old phone, go to Profile and link Sign in with Apple (iPhone) or Google. On the new phone, sign in with the same account from Profile. If you play only as a guest without linking, your progress can’t be moved.

Sign in with Apple is on iPhone only, so it moves your progress to another iPhone.

I bought Remove ads. How do I get it back on a new phone? Remove ads belongs to your Ludo Reign account. If you linked Apple or Google, sign in with it on your new phone and Remove ads comes with it.

If you didn’t link, open the Shop, go to Coins, and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account you bought it with. Restoring this way works only on the same kind of phone: an App Store purchase on an iPhone, a Google Play purchase on Android.

Are the dice fair? Yes. In online games every roll comes from our server and is random. Nothing you buy, and nothing about your account, changes a roll. Computer players are labelled with a bot icon.

A bot took my seat. If you leave a game, or stay disconnected for 30 seconds, a computer player finishes it for you so the others still get a real game. If you reconnect within 30 seconds, you keep your seat. After that you can’t rejoin that game, and you’re placed last.

How do I play with a friend? Tap Friends, make a table and share its six-letter code. Your friend taps Friends and types the code.

How do I delete my account? See Delete your account.
