Ludo Reign Privacy Policy

Effective: 27 October 2026

Ludo Reign is developed and published by Grab It US LLC (shown as ‘Grab It US’ on Google Play). This policy explains what information the Ludo Reign app and its game server handle, why, who else receives it, how long it’s kept, and how to delete it. Questions: ludoreign@nautica.ky.

The short version

  • You can play as a guest. We don’t ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your in-game name is generated for you (for example “SwiftMango21”), and your picture is one of our preset avatars.
  • Ads are shown by Google AdMob. They’re personalised only if you allow it.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time. It’s deleted at once.

What we collect and why

Your game account. When you first open Ludo Reign, the app makes a random ID for your phone, and our server makes a player ID for your account. These IDs don’t come from your phone’s hardware or its advertising ID. We use them to sign you in and keep your progress.

Your profile and progress:

  • your generated name, preset avatar and the country flag you choose. The flag starts from your phone’s region setting, not from GPS, and you can choose “none”;
  • your coins, trophies, Crown XP and level;
  • your game statistics, the items you own and use, your daily bonus streak, and whether you bought Remove ads.

We use these to run the game. Other players in your games can see your name, avatar, flag, level and chosen looks. The weekly ranks show your name, avatar, flag and trophies.

Your games. For each online game, we keep a record of every roll and move, the players and their places, and the rewards paid, so any game can be replayed to check it was fair. A record of every coin change, with its reason, is kept with your account.

vs Computer games. When you win or lose a game against the computer, the app sends the game’s moves to our server to claim the reward.

Linking Apple or Google (optional). If you link a Sign in with Apple or Google account to keep your progress on a new phone, our server stores:

  • the Apple or Google account ID;
  • the email address that Apple or Google provides. With Apple this may be a private relay address.

For Google, our server software may also store the name and profile picture address on your Google account. We never show these in the game. We don’t send marketing or newsletters. We only email that address to answer a request you make, such as checking that a deletion request is yours. For Apple, we keep a token that lets us revoke the link at Apple when you delete your account, as Apple requires.

Purchases. Coin packs and Remove ads are bought through the App Store or Google Play, and checked through RevenueCat, a purchase-processing service. We receive and keep the product, the store’s transaction ID, the date, and whether it was a test purchase. We never see your payment card details. RevenueCat receives your player ID and your store purchase records.

Ads. Ludo Reign shows ads through Google AdMob, with Google’s consent tool. Google’s software in the app collects information from your device to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. That can include:

  • device and advertising identifiers;
  • your IP address, and an approximate location worked out from it;
  • device and app information;
  • how you interact with ads;
  • diagnostic data.

Whether ads are personalised:

  • iPhone: only if you allow tracking in Apple’s prompt; otherwise ads are untargeted.
  • EU and UK: you’re asked for consent before any ads load, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Elsewhere: ads may be personalised where the law doesn’t require consent.

“Watch an ad: +50 coins” is optional. When you watch one, Google confirms the ad to our server so the coins can be paid.

Google’s use of this information is described in Google’s privacy policy and its page on how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Technical logs. Our server keeps short-lived logs to find and fix faults. Some lines contain player IDs. They don’t record your IP address.

What we don’t collect

  • No precise location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • No chat: players talk only with preset phrases and emotes, which aren’t stored.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting tools of our own. If that ever changes, this policy changes first.

Who else receives information

We share information only with the services that run the game for us, and only as described above:

  • DigitalOcean: hosts our game server, in Toronto, Canada.
  • Backblaze: stores encrypted copies of our database, in the United States. Backblaze can’t read them.
  • Google (AdMob): ads, as described above.
  • Apple and Google: sign-in, if you link them; the App Store and Google Play, for purchases.
  • RevenueCat: purchase checks.

We don’t sell your information.

How long we keep it

  • Your account, profile, progress, coins, coin history, items and linked sign-ins: until you delete your account.
  • Weekly ranking entries: until the weekly reset (Monday 00:00 Cayman time, UTC−5, no daylight saving), or until you delete your account.
  • Game records: kept, to check fairness. If you delete your account, your player ID and name in them are replaced, so they no longer link to you.
  • Purchase records: kept for refunds and accounting. A copy with no player ID is kept after you delete your account.
  • Rewarded-ad records: coins, day and time, with no player ID, kept so an ad can’t be paid twice.
  • Server logs: kept for a short time, then replaced by newer logs.
  • Backups: encrypted copies of the whole database are kept for up to about 31 days, then deleted. A deleted account can remain in these copies until they expire. They’re used only to recover the service after a fault.

Deleting your account

In the app: Profile → Delete account, then type DELETE. It happens at once, and removes:

  • your progress, coins, items and name;
  • your weekly ranking entry;
  • your linked sign-ins.

We also revoke your Sign in with Apple link at Apple and delete your RevenueCat record. If those outside steps can’t finish straight away, we keep trying until they do.

If you can’t use the app, see Delete your account.

Children

Ludo Reign is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 is using Ludo Reign, contact us at ludoreign@nautica.ky and we’ll delete the account.

Security

The app talks to our server only over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Our database backups are encrypted with a key our server doesn’t hold. Access to the server is limited to its operator.

Your choices and rights

  • You can change your name (“New name”), avatar and flag, choose whether to link Apple or Google, change your ad consent where it’s offered, and delete your account at any time.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your information, or to object to some uses. To ask, email ludoreign@nautica.ky.

Closed beta

During the closed beta:

  • the app shows Google’s test ads;
  • purchases use the stores’ test systems, so no real money is charged;
  • some features may be unavailable.

Google’s ad software still runs in the app and works as described above, even while it shows test ads.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we’ll also say so in the app.
