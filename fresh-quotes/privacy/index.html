Fresh Quotes: privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Fresh Quotes is a quote widget for the Android home screen, published by Grab It US LLC (shown as "Grab It US" on Google Play).

We collect no data. Fresh Quotes does not send any personal or usage information to Grab It, and we do not collect, share or sell any. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no notifications.

Online quotes. When a widget is set to "Zen (Online, Random)", which is the setting it starts with, the app asks ZenQuotes (zenquotes.io) for a random quote: when you add the widget, when you tap it for another quote, and about once a day. The request asks for a random quote and names the app ("DailyQuoteWidget/1.0"). It contains nothing about you, your device or the quotes you have saved. As with any website or online service, ZenQuotes' server receives the IP address your device connects from. Grab It never receives it. How ZenQuotes handles it is described in ZenQuotes' privacy policy. If you choose one of the built-in categories instead, the quotes come from the app itself and no request is made.

What is stored on your device. Fresh Quotes saves, in its own storage on your device: each widget's theme and quote category, the last quote each widget showed, and the quotes you saved with the heart. Grab It never receives them.

When it is deleted. Removing a widget from your home screen deletes that widget's theme, category and last quote. Your saved quotes stay until you uninstall Fresh Quotes or clear its storage in Android's settings. Uninstalling Fresh Quotes removes everything it stored on your device. Because we hold none of this, there is nothing for us to keep or delete.

Backups. Depending on your device's backup and transfer settings, your phone or tablet may include what Fresh Quotes stores in its own backups or copy it to a new device, as it does for other apps. Those backups are managed by your device's provider (such as Google) under your settings, not by us.

Changes. If this ever changes, we will update this page and the app's store listing before the change reaches you.
