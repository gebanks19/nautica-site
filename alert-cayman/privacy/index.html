Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October, 2026

Alert! Cayman ("Alert!", "we") is a community app for live traffic and safety alerts on Grand Cayman. It is published by GRAB IT US LLC, 407 Lincoln Rd., Suite 6H PMB 1470, Miami Beach, FL 33139 United States. This policy explains what we collect, why, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. Questions: alert@nautica.ky.

The short version

  • You sign in with your mobile number. Other people never see it.
  • Your location is used on your phone to show what's near you. It's sent to us only when you post, confirm, clear or comment, so we can check you're on Grand Cayman and near the incident.
  • Faces and licence plates in photos are blurred on your phone before upload, and photo details (like the camera's location data) are removed.
  • Reports, photos and comments are deleted 7 days after the incident ends. We don't keep a history of your movements.
  • We don't sell your data and there are no ads.

Photos and moderation

Photos are blurred on your phone before they leave it. Depending on the kind of incident, a photo may be checked by a moderator, or by an automated image check (Google Cloud Vision), before others see it. Photos must not show injured people, children or the inside of homes, and moderators remove any that do.

Who processes your data

We use Google services to run Alert!: Firebase (sign-in, database, file storage, notifications, crash reports and analytics), Google Cloud (servers and the automated photo check) and Google Maps (maps, place names and address search). Data is stored on Google servers in the United States. Text messages with sign-in codes are sent by Firebase. We do not share your data with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account at any time in the app: Settings → Delete account. This removes your number, zones, settings and device from Alert!. Posts you made stay up anonymously until they are removed with the incident.
  • Location and notifications can be turned off in your phone's settings. You can still read the map without either.
  • Access, correction or deletion: you can ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct or delete it, by emailing alert@nautica.ky with the mobile number on your account.

Children

Alert! is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
